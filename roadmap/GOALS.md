# NY Film Reviews - Project Goals

## Elevator Pitch
A curated film review platform for New York cinema lovers. Current releases, classics, and everything in between — all from a NYC perspective.

## Primary Goals
1. **Content Quality** - Thoughtful, well-written reviews of films showing in NY theaters
2. **Regular Curation** - Monthly watchlists highlighting themed selections
3. **Community Engagement** - Open contribution model via pull requests

## Technical Goals
- Static site for fast loading and low hosting costs
- Mobile-first responsive design
- SEO-optimized for film search terms
- Easy content management via Markdown

## Success Metrics
| Metric | Target | Timeline |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Published Reviews | 20+ | Month 2 |
| Monthly Watchlists | 1+ | Month 1 |
| Site Visitors | 100/month | Month 3 |
| Community PRs | 5+ | Month 4 |

## Non-Goals
- Not a general movie database (IMDb exists)
- Not professional film criticism
- Not a social network
